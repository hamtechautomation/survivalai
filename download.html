Put it on your device

Pick whichever suits you. Both work with no internet afterwards — no account, no app store.

On a computer or USB stick

  1. Plug in a USB stick. Any will do — Lite needs ~5 MB, Standard about 600 MB of space.
  2. Download a package above. It saves as a single .zip file (check your Downloads folder).
  3. Unzip it. Double-click the .zip — Windows and Mac open it for you. You get a last-light-survival-guide folder.
  4. Drag that whole folder onto your USB stick. Wait for it to finish copying — that's it, it's saved.
  5. Open it anywhere. Plug the stick into any computer, open the folder, and double-click index.html. It opens in the browser and works fully offline, forever.

💡 Keep a copy on the stick and your computer. To give it to someone, just copy the folder across — no installing anything.

🔒 Comfortable with a terminal? sh make-usb.sh /Volumes/MYUSB (inside the unzipped folder) copies the guide and re-reads every byte on the stick against SHA-256 hashes — so a flaky drive is caught now, not the day you need it.

On your phone or tablet (like an app)

Add the guide to your home screen and it opens like an app — all 29 sections, the tools, skills and search, with no signal needed. No app store, no account, about 10 MB.

Step-by-step for your phone ↓

The offline AI assistant and the bundled PDF books need a computer — the phone app gives you the written guide, tools and search offline, which is the core of it.

Install it on your phone

You’re using the installed app. Nothing else to do — check the status line at the bottom of the menu says ready offline.

Safari your browser

  1. Open bunkerbot.co.uk in Safari.
  2. Tap Share — in the bar at the bottom on iPhone, top-right on iPad. Only see ? Tap that first, then Share.
  3. Scroll the list and tap Add to Home Screen . Not there? Scroll to the bottom, tap Edit Actions and add it.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on. Tap Add.

Chrome, Edge or Firefox on iPhone your browser

  1. Tap Share — in Chrome it’s in the address bar, top-right.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer. On older iPhones, use Safari.

Chrome your browser

  1. Open bunkerbot.co.uk in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu at the top-right.
  3. Tap Add to home screen, then Install. Some phones say Install app instead — same thing.

Quicker: if this page shows an Install Last Light button above, just tap it.

Samsung Internet your browser

  1. Tap the menu at the bottom-right.
  2. Tap Add page to → Home screen, then Add. If an install icon shows in the address bar, you can tap that instead.

Firefox your browser

Chrome or Edge your browser

No icon? Chrome: menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app. Edge: menu → Apps → Install this site as an app.

I can’t find “Add to Home Screen” or “Install”

You’re probably in an app’s built-in browser — links opened from Instagram, Facebook, Gmail or the Google app can’t install anything. Tap or and choose Open in Safari / Open in Chrome, then follow the steps. Private / Incognito tabs can’t install either.

The icon opens in the browser instead of like an app

Delete the icon and add it again. On newer iPhones make sure Open as Web App is switched on when you tap Add to Home Screen.

How do I get updates?

Automatically. Whenever the app is opened with internet, it checks for a newer version and saves it in the background; the next launch uses it. Nothing to reinstall.

Will my phone delete it to free up space?

An installed app keeps its saved pages. A site you only visit in a browser tab can have its saved data cleared after a few weeks without use — so do install it rather than relying on a bookmark. Open it now and then to be sure, and keep a second copy on a USB stick.

What’s on the phone version, and what isn’t?

Included offline: every section, the Emergency Card, calculators & tools, skills, checklists and search. Not included: the 33 PDF books (too large — open one while online and it stays saved), the offline maps data, and the Bunker Bot AI, which needs a computer.

Installs the offline AI, auto-picks the right model for your machine (down to old/low-RAM hardware — no need to read a table), and can serve the whole guide to every device on your network. Run these from inside the unzipped folder, in order:

Skip this and Bunker Bot still works — it searches the bundled books and shows you the right page, it just won't generate a written answer.

＋ Add the offline AI manually (optional)

Bunker Bot can run a real AI model fully offline. The easiest way is a llamafile — a single file that is the model and the server, no install, works on Windows/Mac/Linux. It's a separate download (1–3 GB) so the packages above stay small.

1
Download a llamafile — start with Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct (~2.3 GB; or the 1B, ~0.9 GB, for older hardware) from Mozilla's llamafile project or Hugging Face.
2
Make it runnable and start it:chmod +x *.llamafile && ./your-model.llamafile --server --nobrowser
3
Open the guide, click Bunker Bot, choose the Llamafile mode — it connects automatically. Ask away, fully offline.

⚠️ Small models can be wrong, especially on medical questions — Bunker Bot leans on the bundled books to stay grounded, but always verify anything critical.

⌨ Prefer the terminal? (no browser)

The Standard package includes bunkerbot.py — the whole assistant in one zero-dependency Python file, ideal for a headless Raspberry Pi or over SSH. Same book-cited answers, no browser:

python3 bunkerbot.py "how do I stop heavy bleeding"

Auto-detects Ollama or a llamafile; with no model it drops to a fast search-only lookup over the library. Python 3 only — nothing to install.
