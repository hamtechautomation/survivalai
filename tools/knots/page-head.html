Knots & Rope

Six knots do nine-tenths of the work: a loop that won’t slip, a hitch to a post, a line you can tighten, a join for two ropes, a way to haul a load down hard, and a binding for bundles. Learn these six properly, with what each is for and how each fails, before you learn a seventh.
